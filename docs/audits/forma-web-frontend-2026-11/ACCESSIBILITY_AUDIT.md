Same-route cloud recovery can replace the initially prepared error heading after the one-shot navigation observer disconnects. The explicit Skip action now prepares the current `main h1` with `tabIndex=-1` before focusing it. This preserves normal Skip scrolling and moves focus only after the member activates the link. Root reproduced the recovery failure in all three engines, then the same three cases passed in 13.5 seconds. The existing failed-GET regression retains its fresh-GET, no-PUT, no-page-error and unchanged `#account` assertions; it now also activates Skip and verifies the replacement Account heading receives focus. This functional follow-up changes no visual component and does not imply a manual assistive-technology pass.

The final local account matrices passed **development 200/200** and **production-build 200/200** across all five profiles, with zero failures, skips or retries. Each matrix includes seven account-design cases per profile: 35 design executions within its 200 passes. The matrices combine isolated local API/PostgreSQL/mail-sink journeys with intercepted synthetic design/race fixtures; they are not 200 physical-device checks or 200 database transactions. Final visual evidence covers 48 account original/final pairs, and the responsive sweep passed 88/88 states with zero page errors. Earlier failed attempts retain their original outcomes in the historical receipts; the final passes do not relabel them.

[Aggregate CI run 41840783732](https://github.com/Raoof128/forma-workout-planner/actions/runs/41840783732) completed **SUCCESS** for `c247e9e`: both jobs and every browser stage succeeded, including the remote account stages. The completed Verify logs confirm **540 guest-development passes**, **175 production-smoke passes**, **200 account-development passes** and **200 account-production passes**, with no failed, skipped or interrupted final summaries. These CI executions are recorded separately from the completed local account matrices. Receipts and exact evolving CI status are maintained in [test results](TEST_RESULTS.md).
