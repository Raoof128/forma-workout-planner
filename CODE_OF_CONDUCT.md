# Code of conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Everyone who takes part in its issues, pull requests and discussions is expected
to follow it.

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, keep criticism about the work and
not the person, and do not post anyone's private information.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer, Raouf Abedini, at the contact
address in the [legal documents](docs/legal/README.md). Reports are read by the
maintainer only and are kept confidential. Security problems have their own
private route; see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
