# Privacy on this iPhone

Development notice · 23 October 2026

This native FORMA build is an offline guest workout planner. It does not create an
account or sync with the FORMA website. Your routines, sets, workout history,
notes, display name and training preferences are stored in the app's local
workspace. FORMA does not send those records to its operator in this build.

The app includes no advertising, tracking or analytics SDK and requests no
HealthKit, location, camera, microphone, contacts or photo-library permission.
Exercise search runs on the device. Bundled exercise guides retain their source
and licence information.

The workspace uses the iPhone's file protection and is excluded from automatic
device backup by the app. This is not a claim of end-to-end encryption or a
guarantee against access on an unlocked or compromised device. Deleting the app,
losing the device or resetting local training data can remove the only copy.
Export a backup if you want to keep a separate copy.

Export is an action you choose. A backup may contain all your training records
and notes. The Files or sharing destination you select may copy it to iCloud,
another cloud provider or another person. Their handling of that copy is outside
FORMA's local storage controls. Resetting FORMA does not delete exported files.

Opening an external support, source or legal link connects to the relevant
website or email application, which may process ordinary network or contact
information. FORMA does not automatically attach your workout data. Please avoid
including medical details in support messages unless you intend to share them.

The existing FORMA website and its optional account service have a separate
privacy policy. Their cloud account features are not available in this native
development build. Any future native account release needs updated disclosures,
express workout-data consent, account deletion and an independently reviewed
native authentication and synchronization design.
