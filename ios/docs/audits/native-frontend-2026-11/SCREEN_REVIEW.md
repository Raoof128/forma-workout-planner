These are current captures from the synthetic FORMA Audit app, not the October screenshots. [Before dark captures](screenshots/before-dark/manifest.json) record SHA-256, pixel dimensions, target/capture method and originating test.

The initial launch-argument Light override was ignored on this runtime; those dark images are not labelled Light. The audit app's own appearance control is used for further Light captures without changing the phone's global appearance. Final matching captures and comparison are recorded after final tests.

Hosted widget/Live Activity renderer attachments are distinct from SpringBoard installation and system-hosted ActivityKit. Real Home Screen placement is deliberately not automated on the personal device. `--ui-testing` uses inert ActivityKit and notification hosts, so these screen tours cannot establish Lock Screen privacy or actual intent delivery.
